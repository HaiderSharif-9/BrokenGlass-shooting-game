HOW TO PLAY

Move your mouse to aim, and click / tap to fire.
Hit a bottle → +1 bullet.
Hit a can → +2 bullets.
Hit a heart → heal +2 health.
A rare bomb — shoot it toavoid damage.
Every 5th level throws a big bonus target worth extra score.
Chain hits without missing to build a combo — score multiplier goes up.
Every shot costs 1 bullet — hit or miss.
Miss a bottle/can/bomb and it reaches you → damage.
Out of bullets = Game Over. Health hits zero = You Are Dead.
Land enough hits to level up: full reload, more max bullets, faster targets, new scene.
